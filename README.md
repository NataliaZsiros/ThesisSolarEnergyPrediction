This thesis is about predicting solar-energy production and consumption in both business and household cases in Estonia, called prosumers.
Prosumers contribute largely to energy imbalance, when the energy expected to be used doesn't line up with the actual energy used or produced. The data originates from Enefit, one of the biggest energy companies in the Baltic regions and it contains information about weather, location, electricity and gas prices and client-specific information (eg. installed capacity and the type of product).
The problem was originally shared on Kaggle in a competition and many solutions used complex XGBoost and LightGBM models for prediction, however, based on research papers ANN and statistical models are usually used in case of prediction of energy production and consumption. My aim in this thesis to find a more accurate and preferably a simpler model solving this problem. By improving the accuracy of the prediction, the impact of the thesis would be to consequently reduce the imbalance and associated costs in the case of prosumers thus further advocating environmentally friendly energy solutions.
